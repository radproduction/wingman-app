1. Who we are

Wingman is a personal AI assistant ("chief of staff") that you talk to on WhatsApp and in the Wingman app. It is built and operated by Heard Agency, Karachi, Pakistan ("Wingman", "we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your WhatsApp phone number, your name, time zone, working hours, briefing times and the preferences you set (tone, language, how much Wingman may do on its own, quiet hours).
  • Your conversations with Wingman: the messages you send on WhatsApp or in the app, and Wingman's replies.
  • Voice notes, photos and files you send: voice notes are turned into text; photos, screenshots and documents are read so Wingman can answer about them. Photos you ask Wingman to post are stored so they can be published.
  • Places you save: home and office addresses, and your current location only when you choose to share it (used for commute times and "leave by" reminders).
  • Things you tell Wingman to remember, standing rules you set, and automations you create.

Information from accounts you connect (only if you connect them)

  • Google: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Tasks and Google Contacts, and, through a separate consent, Google Health data.
  • Business email (any IMAP/SMTP mailbox you add).
  • Shopify: store, product, order and customer data.
  • Other apps you choose to connect, for example Facebook Pages, Instagram, Outlook, Slack or similar. Wingman only accesses an app after you sign in to it and grant permission.
  • Health and fitness: readings from Google Health, supported wearables (such as Whoop or Oura) or data you send from Apple Health, for example sleep, steps, heart rate and activity.
  • Work attendance: clock-in and clock-out events, if you connect your HR system.

Meetings (only if you turn on the notetaker)

When you enable the meeting notetaker, a Wingman bot joins the meetings you choose, records the audio and video, and produces a transcript, summary and action items. You are responsible for letting other participants know they are being recorded. If you ask, the recording is saved to your own Google Drive.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical logs needed to run and secure the service (for example request times, errors and delivery status of WhatsApp messages).
  • A sign-in token stored on your device so you stay signed in to the app.

The Wingman app does not use advertising identifiers, third-party analytics SDKs or tracking pixels.

Our website and cookies

Our website, imyourwingman.ai, uses cookies only as follows:

  • Essential: we store your cookie choice (the wm_consent cookie, 12 months). Always on.
  • Analytics, only if you allow it: Google Analytics counts visits and the pages viewed, so we can improve the site.
  • Marketing, only if you allow it: the Meta Pixel measures how our ads perform.

Analytics and marketing cookies are off until you choose "Accept all" or switch them on under "Manage" in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings (also in the footer of every page). If you join the waitlist, we collect your first name and email address to tell you when Wingman opens. None of this applies to your conversations or connected accounts.

What Wingman works out from your data

To be useful, Wingman keeps short notes about you: facts you've shared, a profile summary (your work, key people, projects and preferences), patterns such as when you usually start work or pay bills, and notes about the apps you've connected. You can see and clear what Wingman remembers in the app under Settings → Permissions & privacy → Memory.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the features you use: answering questions, briefings and evening wraps, reminders, calendar and email help, tasks, bills, deliveries, travel, health insights, meeting notes, store and social media help, and image creation.
  • To act for you when you ask, for example sending an email, posting to a page or creating an event. Actions that send, publish, delete or spend money are held until you confirm.
  • To send you messages you've asked for on WhatsApp, including sign-in codes and alerts.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix problems and improve reliability.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We process your information because it is needed to provide the service you signed up for, with your consent (for example when you connect an account), and for our legitimate interest in keeping Wingman safe and working. You can withdraw consent at any time by disconnecting an account or deleting your Wingman account.

4. How the AI works with your data

Wingman's replies are generated by AI models. To answer you, the relevant parts of your message and data (for example the email you asked about) are sent to our AI providers for processing. We use their business API services, under which your content is not used to train their models. We do not use your data to train any AI models either.

AI can make mistakes. Check important details, which is one reason Wingman asks before it sends or changes anything on your behalf.

6. Google user data

Wingman's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google data only to provide and improve the user-facing features you request in Wingman.
  • We do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or with your consent.
  • We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and humans do not read it unless you ask us to (for example for support), it is needed for security, or the law requires it.
  • We do not use Google user data to train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

You can remove Wingman's access at any time in the app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, conversations and memory: for as long as your account is active.
  • Synced email, calendar, tasks and other connected data: kept in step with the source while connected; removed when you disconnect or delete your account.
  • Images: deleted automatically after about 60 days.
  • Sign-in codes: expire after 10 minutes.
  • Technical logs: kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting.

8. Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Permissions & privacy → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and the data stored for it, including your messages, memory, connected-account tokens, synced data, meetings and images, and disconnects the apps you linked through Wingman. Any copies left in backups are removed within 30 days.

Other ways: email us at wehearyou.studio@gmail.com from the address on your account. We will confirm when it is done.

You can also clear only what Wingman remembers about you (Settings → Permissions & privacy → Clear my memory), or disconnect a single account, without deleting everything.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can use most of these directly in the app; for anything else, contact us and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access tokens for connected accounts are stored on our servers and are never exposed in the app; passwords for business mailboxes are encrypted. Sign-in uses one-time codes delivered over WhatsApp. Access to our systems is restricted to people who need it to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires.

11. Children

Wingman is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

We and our providers may process your information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the providers' standard contractual clauses.

13. Changes

We may update this policy as Wingman changes. We will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or on WhatsApp before they take effect.
