How we protect your data

Locked with strong encryption

Your sensitive information — like any saved passwords — is scrambled with bank-level encryption while it's stored. Even someone who reached the storage couldn't read it.

Safe while it travels

Everything you send is encrypted as it moves across the internet, so no one in between can read it — the same protection used by online banking.

Your own private space

Your data sits on our own secure, locked-down servers and is kept completely separate from every other user. No customer can ever see another's information.

Secure sign-in

You log in with a one-time code that expires quickly. Your session can be signed out at any time, and only you can get into your account.

You're always in control

Nothing important without your "yes"

Before Wingman sends a message, makes a payment, or deletes anything, it shows you exactly what it's about to do and waits for your approval. You're always the one who decides.

A clear record of everything

Every action Wingman takes is recorded, so you can always look back and see exactly what happened and when.

The assistant never sees your passwords

Any passwords you save stay locked away — even Wingman's AI can't read them. They're only used quietly behind the scenes to do the task you asked for.

Leave whenever you want

You can disconnect your connected accounts, or delete your data entirely, at any time. It's your data — you stay in charge of it.

Our privacy promise

  • We never sell your data — to anyone.
  • Your conversations are never used to train AI models.
  • If you connect Gmail or Calendar, it's used only for what you ask — never for advertising.
  • You can disconnect or delete your data at any time.
